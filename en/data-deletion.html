Data Deletion

Last updated: April 26, 2026

We respect your right to privacy and control over your data, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), and Meta platform policies. You can request the deletion of all data we store at any time.

What we delete

  • Instagram access token
  • Instagram user ID and username
  • Cached feed (Redis)
  • Registration information linked to the account

Timeframe

Data is deleted within 30 days after request confirmation. You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete.

Revoking access via Instagram

You can also revoke our application's access directly through Instagram, without needing to contact us:

  1. Open Instagram and go to Profile → Menu → Settings
  2. Navigate to Security → Apps and websites
  3. Find InstagramWidget and tap Remove

This revokes the access token immediately. Any other data stored by us will be deleted within 30 days.
